# Code of Conduct

Project Ambient follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1.

We pledge to make participation harassment-free regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, constructive feedback, respect for different experiences, and accepting responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention, threats, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing private information without permission.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that violate these standards, and may temporarily or permanently ban a participant when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository’s security advisory channel, marking the report “Code of Conduct.” Maintainers will preserve confidentiality as far as practical and respond proportionately.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
