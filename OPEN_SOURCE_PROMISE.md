# Open-Source Promise

Project Ambient’s local library management, rules engine, renderer adapters, channel manifest, local CLI, and local MCP controls will remain open source.

We will not put privacy, accessibility, security fixes, restore behavior, or power-saving controls behind a paywall. Local use will not require an account. Telemetry will remain off by default. The project will not sell user data, place ads in the wallpaper experience, accept pay-to-rank content, or bias recommendations for affiliate revenue.

Optional paid products may fund hosting, encrypted sync, managed fleets, commercial support, or explicitly licensed creator packs. A direct open-source local build will remain available, and paid creator content must show provenance and creator economics clearly.
