# Open-Source Promise

Project Ambient's useful local product remains available without an account under the MIT License.

The Community core includes local import/reference, catalog and duplicate tools, playlists and packs, rules and policy, static/hybrid/live platform adapters, multi-display continuity, schedules and solar rules, lifecycle and energy controls, recovery, accessibility, privacy/security fixes, local diagnostics, CLI/App Intents/MCP contracts, deterministic in-app chat, and adapters for user-operated self-hosted providers.

We will not:

- paywall privacy, accessibility, security fixes, restore, provenance, or power-saving controls;
- silently upload, train on, sell, redistribute, or claim ownership of user media or prompts;
- require an account for local use;
- move an existing MIT component behind a commercial license without a new public decision and contributor review;
- add ads to the wallpaper experience, accept pay-to-rank content, or bias recommendations for affiliate revenue;
- make model-generated text a privileged path around typed commands, previews, verification, or undo.

Optional paid products may fund hosted encrypted sync, managed fleets, SSO/SCIM, organization policy, longer hosted audit retention, service-level commitments, enterprise support, or separately licensed creator packs. Service-specific code or genuinely commercial managed modules may have a separate license and package boundary, but the Community product must remain complete and interoperable through published contracts.

Software licensing never overrides media, model, provider, trademark, privacy, or publicity rights. Creator content keeps its own license and economics. Contributions to MIT components use inbound-equals-outbound MIT with DCO sign-off; any future CLA, dual-license, or relicensing proposal requires a separate public decision and qualified legal review.

See `docs/decisions/0002-commercial-rights.md` for the enforceable boundary.
